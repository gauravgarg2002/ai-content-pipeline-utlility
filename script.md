## Scan before you steal

**3.** Step one is the Waink app on your phone. Open your phone with up on the d-pad, then scan a car. It shows the car's stats, its condition, and its security, including alarms and trackers. [1, 2]
   _visual: phone in hand, parked sports car_ · callout: **WAINK SCANNER**

**4.** It also tells you what the car sells for and what it costs to register. That one scan tells you whether a car is a quick payday, a keeper, or a waste of your time. [2]
   _visual: luxury cars parked on a street_ · callout: **SELL PRICE · REGISTER COST**

**5.** And if you're already driving, press down to check for a tracker. Get in the habit of checking before you get attached to a car. [1]
   _visual: driving POV, dashboard_ · callout: **PRESS DOWN = TRACKER CHECK**

**6.** Two more Waink tricks most players miss. Stand near a car you've already been in and press L1 to see its engine details. And while driving, hold left on the d-pad to bring the car's info up on your screen. [1]
   _visual: close-up of dashboard and HUD_ · callout: **L1 = ENGINE · HOLD LEFT = INFO**

## Every way into a car

**7.** Now, getting in. First, just try the door. Some parked cars are unlocked, and that's the quietest theft in the game. [1]
   _visual: character opening a car door_ · callout: **TRY THE DOOR**

**8.** If it's locked, you can smash the window. It's fast, but it can trigger the alarm, and an alarm alerts the people nearby. [1, 2]
   _visual: smashing a car window, alarm_ · callout: **SMASH = ALARM RISK**

**9.** Locked cars also need hotwiring. Push both sticks together to fill the semi-circles and the engine starts. Most older cars can be hotwired. [1, 2]
   _visual: hotwiring a car, engine starting_ · callout: **HOTWIRE: BOTH STICKS**

**10.** High-end keyless cars are different. You can't hotwire them. You need a key cloner, which unlocks later in the story through Randy, the contact who hands out high-end car contracts. [1, 2]
   _visual: modern supercar, keyless entry_ · callout: **KEYLESS = KEY CLONER**

**11.** And of course, you can still carjack. Pull the driver out and the car is yours, at least for now. [1]
   _visual: carjacking a driver on the street_ · callout: **CARJACK**

## Trackers: the trap

**12.** Here's the trap I mentioned. There are two kinds of tracker. A standard tracker just stops you from modifying the car until it's registered to you. [1, 2]
   _visual: car in a mod shop_ · callout: **STANDARD TRACKER**

**13.** An advanced tracker is the dangerous one. It's common on high-end cars, it notifies the police when the car is stolen, and it reports the car's location unless you're moving at high speed. [1, 2]
   _visual: police cars chasing a supercar_ · callout: **ADVANCED TRACKER = COPS**

**14.** Red Bull's guide adds that an advanced tracker pings the police once the car gets too far from where you took it. So the longer and slower your drive home, the worse it gets. Either drive fast, or don't take it in the first place. [2, 1]
   _visual: police helicopter tracking a car_ · callout: **FAR + SLOW = PINGED**

**15.** So if you steal a tracked supercar, keep your foot down. Slow down in traffic and you're broadcasting your position. [1, 2]
   _visual: high speed highway chase_ · callout: **KEEP MOVING**

**16.** In Chapter 1 you can still drive other cars around Leonida, just only temporarily. So enjoy them, but don't get attached until registration unlocks. [3]
   _visual: driving different cars around the Keys_ · callout: **CHAPTER 1 = TEMPORARY**

## Keep it forever

**17.** Now the part most people miss: keeping a car. Registration unlocks in Chapter 2, at Moses's garage in Port Gellhorn, after his introductory mission. Before that, in Chapter 1, Jason's starting car is your main personal vehicle. [3, 4]
   _visual: garage in Port Gellhorn, driving in_ · callout: **REGISTER: CHAPTER 2**

**18.** The process is simple. Drive a car without a tracker into Moses's workshop, choose Respray, pick a new colour, then choose Register. Your first registration during that mission is free, so pick a car you actually love. [3, 4]
   _visual: car being resprayed in a garage_ · callout: **RESPRAY → REGISTER**

**19.** After that, registering costs money. The Waink scan shows you the price before you steal, and cheaper cars cost less to register. [2]
   _visual: money and car keys_ · callout: **WORSE CAR = CHEAPER**

**20.** Pay N Spray can register cars too, and it can sell them for cash. But depending on the tracker, some vehicles get refused. [1, 2]
   _visual: Pay N Spray garage exterior_ · callout: **PAY N SPRAY**

**21.** Why does registering matter so much? Registered cars can be modified, and if you lose one, you can always get it back. An unregistered stolen car that gets destroyed is gone for good. [1]
   _visual: car exploding, then garage_ · callout: **REGISTERED = RECOVERABLE**

**22.** Found something special but can't afford to register it yet? The safest move is to park it in your garage and leave it there until you have the cash. Driving it around before that puts it at risk. [3, 6]
   _visual: parking a supercar in a garage_ · callout: **PARK IT, DON'T DRIVE IT**

**23.** Once a car is registered, the Scooter Bros app lets you summon it any time. And your personal vehicle carries spare outfits in its trunk, which is a lifesaver when the police are looking for your clothes. [2, 6]
   _visual: car pulling up, trunk opening_ · callout: **SCOOTER BROS = SUMMON**

## Garages

**24.** You also need space. Every registered car needs a free garage spot. Garages store your cars for free, hold your weapon loadout, and let you drop off illegal goods. [2, 4]
   _visual: garage interior with cars_ · callout: **NEED A FREE SPOT**

**25.** More space comes as the story moves on, including Valentina's garage later in the game. So if your slots are full, keep playing the main missions before you start selling off your favourites. [7]
   _visual: large garage with several cars_ · callout: **STORY UNLOCKS MORE SLOTS**

**26.** You can only buy garages after Jason and Lucia leave Key Lento, and buying every garage unlocks an achievement called Little Boxes. So start saving early if you're a collector. [2, 7]
   _visual: Vice City streets, garage doors_ · callout: **ACHIEVEMENT: LITTLE BOXES**

**27.** And for repairs and customisation there are Pay N Spray locations all over the map, nineteen of them according to MetaForge's count, so you're never far from a fix. [7]
   _visual: car being repaired and customised_ · callout: **19 PAY N SPRAYS**

## The best cars

**28.** Now, which cars are worth a garage spot? A quick warning first: Rockstar hasn't published top speeds, so instead I'll use the speed ratings the game itself shows, as recorded by GamerBlurb. [5]
   _visual: row of sports cars_ · callout: **GAME'S OWN RATINGS**

**29.** The highest speed rating so far belongs to the Pegassi Infernus 96, at 7.1 out of 10. It's the Lamborghini Diablo-style exotic, and right now it's the pick for raw speed. [5]
   _visual: supercar accelerating_ · callout: **INFERNUS '96 · 7.1/10**

**30.** Next is the Grotti Cheetah 95 at 6.6, but that one is an Ultimate Edition car, so not everyone has it. Then the Pfister Comet 88 at 6.1, the classic Porsche 911-style sports car, and a great all-rounder for everyday driving. [5]
   _visual: classic sports cars driving_ · callout: **CHEETAH 6.6 · COMET 6.1**

**31.** For classic style, the Invetero Coquette 64 rates 5.9, the Corvette C2 look. The Annis Elegy 90 and the Bravado Buffalo 19 both sit at 5.5, so you get a Nissan Skyline-style car or a Dodge Charger-style four-door muscle car with the same speed. [5]
   _visual: classic and muscle cars_ · callout: **COQUETTE 5.9 · ELEGY 5.5**

**32.** Muscle fans get the Bravado Gauntlet Custom 69 at 5.4, the Dodge Challenger lookalike. For everyday driving, the Pfister Astron, a Porsche Macan-style SUV, and the Obey 8F Drafter, an Audi RS5-style coupe, both rate 5.3. [5]
   _visual: muscle car burnout, SUV_ · callout: **GAUNTLET 5.4 · ASTRON 5.3**

**33.** And if you go off-road, especially in the Grassrivers or up near Mount Kalaga, the BF Bandito 77 is slow on the road, but its off-road score is far higher than its road score. [5]
   _visual: off-road buggy in swamp and forest_ · callout: **OFF-ROAD: BANDITO '77**

**34.** Don't forget Jason's own car, the Vapid Ganado 70. It's no speed machine at 4.7, but it's his personal vehicle from the start and it's free. [5]
   _visual: pickup truck in the Keys_ · callout: **GANADO '70 · FREE**

**35.** One more thing: cars in GTA 6 need fuel, and electric ones need charging. A supercar with an empty tank is just an expensive statue, so keep an eye on it before a big job. [1]
   _visual: gas station at night_ · callout: **FUEL MATTERS NOW**

## Keep or sell?

**36.** Not every car should be kept. Moses also buys stolen cars, but only ones without a tracker, and after a sale he goes on cooldown. Sleep at a safehouse and the cooldown resets. [6]
   _visual: garage handover, character sleeping_ · callout: **MOSES: NO TRACKERS**

**37.** Pay N Spray buys cars too, but it has a daily limit and won't take high-end cars with trackers. And later, Randy's high-end contracts pay the most of all. [6]
   _visual: Pay N Spray, luxury car delivery_ · callout: **RANDY PAYS MOST**

**38.** So the rule is simple: tracker-free and fun to drive, keep it. Tracked, or just average, sell it. [1, 6]
   _visual: split screen garage vs fence_ · callout: **KEEP FUN · SELL THE REST**

## Right car for the job

**39.** Your car choice matters for crimes too. Older cars you can hotwire are safer for jobs than luxury cars, which need a cloned key and often carry trackers. [6]
   _visual: old sedan at a robbery scene_ · callout: **CRIMES: OLD CARS**

**40.** For robberies, think about space. Each character carries two stash bags, a van or truck holds up to eight, and a motorbike holds none. Bring a bike to a jewellery job and you're leaving money behind. [2]
   _visual: loading bags into a van_ · callout: **VAN = 8 BAGS · BIKE = 0**

**41.** But for escaping at three stars or more, a normal car struggles. A fast bike, or going on foot through alleys, works better. [6]
   _visual: motorbike weaving through traffic_ · callout: **3+ STARS: TAKE A BIKE**

## My advice

**42.** So here's my advice. Don't burn your free registration on the first shiny car you see. Scan a few, check for trackers, and register something fast and tracker-free. [3, 4]
   _visual: character scanning cars in a lot_ · callout: **DON'T WASTE THE FREE ONE**

**43.** Sell the tracked cars instead of keeping them, use older hotwireable cars for crimes, and keep your favourite registered car clean for driving around. [1, 2]
   _visual: selling a car at a garage_ · callout: **SELL TRACKED · KEEP CLEAN**

## 3 car mistakes

**44.** And three mistakes to avoid. Mistake one: using your favourite registered car for a robbery. If the police spot it, the car becomes part of your heat, and the only fix is to ditch it or switch cars. [6]
   _visual: registered sports car at a crime scene, police_ · callout: **MISTAKE 1: CRIMES IN YOUR FAVE**

**45.** Mistake two: smashing windows in busy streets. The alarm alerts everyone nearby, and witnesses are how the police find out about you in the first place. [1, 2]
   _visual: crowded street, alarm going off_ · callout: **MISTAKE 2: ALARMS IN CROWDS**

**46.** Mistake three: falling in love with a supercar before you scan it. If it has an advanced tracker, you can't simply keep it, and you've just painted a target on yourself. [1, 2]
   _visual: supercar with police lights behind_ · callout: **MISTAKE 3: NOT SCANNING**

**47.** Quick recap. Scan with Waink. Try the door before smashing the window. Watch out for advanced trackers. Register in Chapter 2 at Moses's garage, your first one is free. And you need a free garage spot for every car you keep. [1, 2, 3, 4]
   _visual: fast recap montage_ · callout: **SCAN → STEAL → REGISTER**
