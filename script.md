## The literal easter egg

**3.** Players found a bird's nest full of colourful eggs at the top of a tower in Leonida, reportedly a radio or communications tower. The climb takes about seven minutes. [2]
   _visual: tall tower against the sky_ · callout: **A NEST ON A TOWER**

**4.** Take one of the eggs and throw it toward the ground, and a short cutscene plays showing the egg smashing onto the windscreen of a car driving below. [2]
   _visual: egg falling, car windscreen_ · callout: **THROW IT DOWN**

**5.** The footage was shared on X by a player called xGarbett on November 18th, right after the game's staggered release. You can even talk to an influencer at the top of the tower. The exact tower isn't confirmed in Rockstar's materials, so treat the location as player-reported. [2]
   _visual: influencer on a rooftop_ · callout: **PLAYER-REPORTED**

**6.** It's a nod to a classic: the original Vice City had a hidden room with an actual chocolate easter egg inside. Rockstar has been doing this for over twenty years. [2]
   _visual: art deco building interior_ · callout: **VICE CITY DID IT FIRST**

## Tommy Vercetti's ghost

**7.** Tommy Vercetti isn't in GTA 6, but his name is. Two revolvers in the Ultimate Edition, the Hawk and the Little Morgan, are listed as sourced from the Vercetti Estate, with palm-tree engravings on the grips. [1]
   _visual: revolver close-up, palm trees_ · callout: **SOURCED FROM THE VERCETTI ESTATE**

**8.** And promo material shows Jason and Lucia outside the Ocean View Hotel, which was Tommy's very first safehouse in the 2002 game. That one is a deliberate callback. [1]
   _visual: pastel art deco hotel_ · callout: **OCEAN VIEW HOTEL**

**9.** Fan theory territory: in the second trailer, a painting of an iguana appears to be wearing Tommy's famous tropical shirt. Fans spotted it, but Rockstar hasn't confirmed it. [1]
   _visual: iguana on a rock_ · callout: **THEORY: TOMMY'S SHIRT**

**10.** Why does this matter? Because it confirms that Vice City's history still exists in this universe. Tommy built an empire here, and decades later his estate is selling off guns. That's a great bit of world-building for anyone who played the original. [1]
   _visual: old mansion gates, overgrown_ · callout: **TOMMY'S LEGACY LIVES ON**

## The music

**11.** Flash FM is back. It was a radio station in the original Vice City, and Rockstar has confirmed its return. [1]
   _visual: car radio at night, neon_ · callout: **FLASH FM RETURNS**

**12.** Slayer's Raining Blood is back too. It played on V-Rock in the original Vice City, and in GTA 6 it shows up on The Chamber 106.6, a station hosted by Slayer members as DJ Tom and DJ Kerry. [1]
   _visual: rock station vibe, driving fast_ · callout: **THE CHAMBER 106.6**

**13.** And Phil Collins' Against All Odds appears in the extended promo footage. Collins was in Vice City Stories, where he played a virtual concert at Hyman Memorial Stadium. [1]
   _visual: stadium at night_ · callout: **PHIL COLLINS CALLBACK**

## Phil Cassidy?

**14.** In the second trailer, a man fires a weapon under a sign that reads Phil's Ammu-Nation. Some fans think it's Phil Cassidy from Vice City, or a relative. Again, Rockstar hasn't confirmed it, so file this under theory. [1]
   _visual: gun range, man firing_ · callout: **THEORY: PHIL CASSIDY**

**15.** If you never played the original: Phil Cassidy was Vice City's one-armed arms dealer, who ran his own gun range out in the country. A Phil's Ammu-Nation is exactly the kind of joke Rockstar loves, which is why fans want this one to be real.
   _visual: rural gun range_ · callout: **WHO WAS PHIL?**

## Liberty City and beyond

**16.** Here's a confirmed one from the official character profiles. Lucia's mother wanted a good life for her ever since their days in Liberty City, the setting of GTA 3 and GTA 4. [1]
   _visual: Lucia close-up_ · callout: **LUCIA'S LIBERTY CITY PAST**

**17.** And players have found books in the game that mention Red Dead Redemption and GTA 4. Rockstar keeps hinting that its worlds are connected. [6]
   _visual: bookshelf, interior details_ · callout: **BOOKS NAME OTHER GAMES**

**18.** Another Red Dead link, this one a theory: during a TV scene with Jason, there's footage of animals grazing by a river just before a Prairie Sandwich Meal ad. Reddit users think that landscape comes from Red Dead Redemption 2. [1]
   _visual: TV screen in a living room_ · callout: **THEORY: RDR2 ON TV**

**19.** And a sticker in the first trailer appears to show Everett Morgan, a Red Dead Online character. Also unconfirmed, but it fits the pattern. [1]
   _visual: car bumper sticker_ · callout: **THEORY: EVERETT MORGAN**

**20.** Going back even further, the launch trailer shows North Fort Law. Longtime fans will recognise it as the military base from the very first GTA, in the State of New Guernsey. [3]
   _visual: military base gates_ · callout: **NORTH FORT LAW · GTA 1**

**21.** The trailer also leaves an open question there: we don't know if that's a flashback to Jason's army days or a present-day mission where he calls in old squadmates. TechWiser flagged it, and nobody can say for sure yet. [3]
   _visual: soldiers, Jason in uniform-like gear_ · callout: **FLASHBACK OR MISSION?**

## Brands and parodies

**22.** The classic brands are back. Weazel News appears to report on a police chase in the first trailer. Sessanta Nove, the fake luxury fashion label from GTA 5, returns too. [1]
   _visual: news broadcast, fashion store_ · callout: **WEAZEL NEWS · SESSANTA NOVE**

**23.** And here's one that escaped the game entirely: fans spotted real-life Weazel News ads in Miami. Rockstar's fake news network showed up in the real city that inspired Vice City. [1]
   _visual: Miami street billboard style shot_ · callout: **WEAZEL NEWS IN REAL MIAMI**

**24.** In extended footage, there's a Fleeca Bank sticker on Jason's fridge. And the launch trailer has a fried chicken bucket parody that fans linked to a well-known fast food chain. [1, 4]
   _visual: fridge door, fast food bucket_ · callout: **FLEECA ON THE FRIDGE**

**25.** Fleeca, if you don't remember, was the bank chain from GTA 5, the one behind the Fleeca Job heist. Seeing it on Jason's fridge is a quiet way of saying Los Santos and Leonida share the same world.
   _visual: bank branch exterior_ · callout: **FLEECA = GTA 5 BANK**

**26.** And the '95 Grotti Cheetah is a modern take on the classic Cheetah sports car, with a retro-futuristic livery. It's one of the Ultimate Edition cars. [1]
   _visual: retro sports car_ · callout: **'95 GROTTI CHEETAH**

**27.** Car fans also spotted a supercar in the launch trailer with a Ferrari SF90-style shape. Rockstar always parodies real cars, and GTA 6 has some of its closest lookalikes yet. [4]
   _visual: sleek red supercar_ · callout: **SF90-STYLE SUPERCAR**

**28.** And honestly, the parodies are half the fun. Rockstar's sense of humour is sharpest when it's poking at real brands, and Leonida is clearly packed with them.
   _visual: billboards and shop signs_ · callout: **PARODY EVERYWHERE**

## Small details

**29.** Now the small stuff that makes Leonida feel alive. Weapons stay visible on your character even when you're sitting in a car as a passenger. [4]
   _visual: passenger with rifle visible_ · callout: **GUNS STAY VISIBLE**

**30.** Firearms have serial numbers, and some of them reference previous GTA games. Rockstar hid easter eggs inside the guns themselves. [5]
   _visual: close-up of weapon_ · callout: **SERIAL NUMBER EASTER EGGS**

**31.** The launch trailer also shows rideable electric scooters, multiple jet skis, and what looks like a returning blimp. Fans are hoping that blimp is flyable again. [4, 3]
   _visual: jet skis on water, blimp in sky_ · callout: **SCOOTERS · JET SKIS · BLIMP**

**32.** TechWiser spotted a Komodo dragon hiding in a swamp scene in the trailer, and a Marauder print on a shirt that matches an arm tattoo, possibly a hint at Jason's army past. [3]
   _visual: swamp, reptile in reeds_ · callout: **KOMODO IN THE SWAMP**

**33.** And the six-star wanted level is back for the first time since GTA 4. GTA 5 capped it at five. [1]
   _visual: police HUD with stars_ · callout: **6 STARS RETURN**

**34.** Six stars was last seen in GTA 4 and Chinatown Wars, so bringing it back is a callback in itself, and a warning about how serious the police are this time. [1]
   _visual: SWAT vans, helicopter_ · callout: **LAST SEEN: GTA IV**

**35.** Even the wildlife has secrets. The in-game wildlife chart lists alligators, boars, bobcats, snakes and more, and it has a separate symbol for toxic waste. Somewhere in Leonida, something is glowing. [8]
   _visual: swamp, mysterious green water_ · callout: **TOXIC WASTE ON THE CHART**

**36.** Flamingos and alligators share the Grassrivers, and the underwater areas hold treasure and secrets of their own. If a place looks empty, it usually isn't. [8]
   _visual: flamingos, underwater reef_ · callout: **SECRETS UNDERWATER**

## Hidden in your own house

**37.** There are secrets in Jason's own house, too. Under the loose floorboards in his bedroom, where you can stash cash, you'll find old photos that add to his backstory. [7]
   _visual: bedroom floor, old photographs_ · callout: **PHOTOS UNDER THE FLOOR**

**38.** And meet Dolores, Jason's pet snake. Feed her and you get a small early Focus boost. It's a tiny detail, but it tells you how much Rockstar wanted this world to feel lived in. [7]
   _visual: snake in a terrarium_ · callout: **FEED DOLORES**

**39.** Gloriana is another fun one. People speculated it was a whole new state, but it appears to be just a single key island. [8]
   _visual: small island in the Keys_ · callout: **GLORIANA = AN ISLAND**

## How to find more

**40.** Want to find your own? Use Focus Mode. It highlights interactables in yellow, so hidden notes and objects stand out. [7]
   _visual: focus vision, yellow highlights_ · callout: **FOCUS FINDS SECRETS**

**41.** Check the minimap inside shops for back rooms and staircases, and look for dark blue buildings on the map, because those have interiors. Most secrets are inside, not on the street. [7]
   _visual: map with dark blue buildings_ · callout: **LOOK INSIDE**

**42.** And climb things. The egg was at the top of a seven-minute climb. If Rockstar put a long climb somewhere, there's usually a reason. [2]
   _visual: climbing a tower ladder_ · callout: **CLIMB EVERYTHING**

**43.** And remember the scale here: more than seven hundred enterable interiors have been reported. Rockstar hid that egg in one tower. Imagine what's sitting in the rest. [8]
   _visual: interior montage: bars, motels, shops_ · callout: **700+ PLACES TO HIDE THINGS**
